## The one-sentence map

- **iCanStudy is encoding-first** (how to *build* knowledge: schemas, inquiry, layers).
- **The Art of Memory is storage-and-retrieval-first** (how to *hold and recover* it: loci, striking images, order).
- **Human-optimization research is the outer constraint** (sleep, nutrition, recovery, stimulation — the physiology that makes the other two work).

**The full stack** (the user's framing, and historically accurate — the art was never standalone: fourth canon of rhetoric, part of Prudence, Renaissance world-models): **build → hold → sustain.** iCanStudy builds the schema, the classical art holds and recovers it, the physiology sustains the learner. See `egypt-and-origins-note.md` §3.

## Link discipline

Every cross-reference is tagged **direct** (same mechanism), **convergent** (independent arrival), or **conceptual** (analogous, not demonstrated). Conceptual links are hypotheses, not findings. Never claim one body validates another without a direct link.

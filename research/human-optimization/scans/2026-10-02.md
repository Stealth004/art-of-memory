## Bottom line

The verifiable public record shows a large, unclassified U.S. military research portfolio across learning, cognition, and physical performance — but it is **restorative/optimizing, not "super soldier."** No verified record exists of any operational soldier-enhancement program. Social-media "super soldier" claims are speculation.

## Learning & Memory

- **DARPA TNT** (Targeted Neuroplasticity Training, 2016–~2021, PM Doug Weber): vagal/trigeminal nerve stimulation to boost synaptic plasticity during training; target ≥30% learning-rate improvement. 8 teams, 7 institutions.
- **TNT headline result (2024):** *Scientific Reports*, July 26, 2024 — randomized, double-blind tcVNS trial (n=36) at the Defense Language Institute: improved Arabic vocabulary recall vs sham (P=.025), carryover on unstimulated day 5, plus focus/energy gains. Author Richard McKinley (AFRL 711th HPW) called it "the first randomized, double-blind sham-controlled trial demonstrating the ability of nVNS to accelerate the learning of Arabic vocabulary in students at the Defense Language Institute."
- **DARPA RAM** (2013–, Kahana/Rizzuto): closed-loop stimulation when memory predicted to fail (Ezzyat et al., *Current Biology* 2017); hippocampal CA3→CA1 prosthesis up to ~37% short-term memory gain (Hampson et al. ~2018); ~19% boost in TBI patients (2023); ~28% in epilepsy cohort (2024).
- **DARPA AugCog** (2001–~2006): closed-loop interfaces adapting displays to operator cognitive state (Lockheed Martin PACE, 2006).
- Open: whether TNT met its 30% benchmark (no public final report); N3 status.

## Cognitive Performance

- **WRAIR Sleep Research Center:** 2B-Alert app (personalized caffeine dosing from PVT data; validated in 62-h sleep-deprivation study); **sleep-banking doctrine** (each banked hour in prior 2 weeks boosts effectiveness); SAFTE/UMP model (FAA-approved).
- **Stimulants:** USAF authorizes dextroamphetamine + modafinil as voluntary "go pills" for selected aircrew (≤400 mg/24h per Moody AFBI 44-101, 2010). Dutch AF RCTs (2023–24): modafinil and caffeine both improve vigilance; modafinil cut recovery sleep 30% without efficiency loss.
- **tDCS (AFRL 711 HPW):** vigilance-decrement mitigation in controlled studies (DTIC 2015); no fielded system.
- **Army CSF2** resilience program (est. 2008, renamed 2012): active.

## Athleticism & Physical Performance

- **Army H2F** (since 2021, five domains): Sept. 17, 2026 *JAMA Network Open* study of 1.42M injuries — 5.9% less limited-duty time, ~5.7 fewer restricted days by 2025.
- **Exoskeletons:** TALOS (SOCOM, 2013–2019) concluded infeasible (~$80M); ONYX (Lockheed Martin/B-Temia) in Army evaluation since 2018; Army Futures Command drafting formal requirement (2020) — no fielded system.
- **USARIEM:** 10–14-day heat acclimatization doctrine; Heat Injury Prevention System wearable (14,000 troops' data since 2018, Marine Corps use since 2021); carb supplementation sustains performance in caloric deficit; Ranger-training 1,000 kcal/day deficit study (Baker et al. 2025).

## Speculation flags

- "Super soldier" social-media/YouTube claims (skill "downloading," "300% faster Mandarin," secret genetic/pharmacological enhancement): no primary record.
- MKUltra: real and declassified, but an interrogation/coercion program — not warfighter enhancement.
- "Go pill" folklore: policy confirms regulated, voluntary use — not mass dosing.
- Manufacturer PR (e.g., electroCore on the tcVNS paper): treat the paper, not the PR, as the record.

## Cross-references to Art of Memory (see CONSOLIDATED.md)

- tcVNS Arabic-vocabulary trial → paired-associate recall is exactly what classical mnemonics train (Ad Herennium *imagines agentes*; retrieval practice, Roediger & Karpicke 2006).
- RAM closed-loop (stimulate only when encoding predicted to fail; stimulation during good encoding was disruptive) → desirable difficulties (Bjork 1994): effort matters.
- Sleep banking / 2B-Alert → sleep and consolidation (Diekelmann & Born 2010).
- AugCog adaptive interfaces → cognitive load theory (Sweller 1988).
- H2F five domains → the recovery architecture (sleep, nutrition) that enables intensive cognitive training; spacing effect (Cepeda et al. 2006) for training schedules.
- No military study in this scan tests method of loci or spaced repetition directly — overlaps are conceptual, not demonstrated. Do not claim these programs validate classical mnemonics.

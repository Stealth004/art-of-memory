## Learning & Memory

- **DARPA Firefox — NEW PROGRAM (verified on darpa.mil, solicitation DARPA-PS-26-137).** BTO program announced via Proposers Day notice (DARPA-SN-26-112, Aug 10, 2026). Goal: non-invasive BCI using targeted light beams to detect microscopic movements of neural membranes as they fire — sidestepping the precision/invasiveness trade-off. 36 months, two technical areas (dynamic optical scanners across multiple brain regions; analog pre-digitization processing), government IV&V benchmarked against fMRI in human subjects. Target: 100-channel BCI, 50-micron spatial / 100-microsecond temporal resolution. Partially answers open question #3 (N3 status): N3's program page now reads "complete / no longer maintained" with DARPA telling press the teams will publish details in 2026; Firefox is the new BTO non-invasive BCI direction.
- **NRx Defense Systems — $11,177,757.27 DARPA prime contract (announced Sept 30, 2026, via company GlobeNewswire release — treat as PR, not a peer-reviewed record).** Funds the SPARC-TMS trial (clinicaltrials.gov NCT07227103): single-day full TMS course + NRX-101 (d-cycloserine NMDA partial agonist + lurasidone raising NMDA receptor expression) for major depressive disorder. Sites: McLean Hospital (Harvard), ASU, HOPE Therapeutics, with expansion planned to leading military hospitals. Therapeutic/restorative indication, not enhancement — but the mechanism (NMDA-mediated synaptic plasticity) is the same cellular machinery TNT targeted for learning. Worth watching: if results publish, it bears directly on the plasticity-enhancement evidence base.
- **D-PECHE (backfill — missed by earlier scans; announced via DARPA special notice ~May 2026).** DNA-Protein Epigenetic Chemistry Engineering: reversible "new-to-nature" DNA base modifications for "cognitive enhancement, biothreat protection, regenerative medicine." Seen only in secondary outlets so far (Activist Post, YogaEsoteric quoting the notice); not yet verified against a DARPA primary source. Added to program table with that caveat.
- **Speculation flag:** YouTube video "DARPA's New Invention Could Replace Studying Forever" (The Pushpak, ~8 days old) claims TNT test subjects "learned Mandarin Chinese tonal grammar 300% faster." Unsupported — no primary record; TNT's verified headline remains the 2024 Arabic-vocabulary trial (P=.025), and the ≥30% figure was a program target, not a demonstrated outcome.

## Cognitive Performance

- **Caffeine meta-analysis — NEW PEER-REVIEWED (Frontiers in Nutrition, published Oct 1, 2026; PROSPERO CRD420261375742).** Systematic review + meta-analysis of 7 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials in military personnel under sleep deprivation/restriction. Effect sizes (SMD): vigilance/response 0.73 (95% CI 0.50–0.96, I²=0%); complex cognition 0.54 (0.16–0.93); military task performance 0.45 (0.19–0.71). Largest, most consistent gains on simple vigilance/speed; complex-task and precision-shooting gains smaller and task-dependent. Conclusion: caffeine is a practical short-term adjunct, not a stand-alone strategy; dose/timing still need individual optimization. This quantitatively tightens the existing WRAIR/2B-Alert and USAF "go pill" entries.
- **Havana Syndrome cognitive rehab (announced Sept 29, 2026 via advocacy-tinged GlobeNewswire wire).** Retrospective observational study: 40 patients (2020–2025) at Walter Reed's Brain Fitness Center did BrainHQ 20–30 min/session, 3–5×/week for 6 weeks; ANAM4 MIL composite scores improved. Small, observational, PR-framed — restorative, not enhancement; one-line record only.

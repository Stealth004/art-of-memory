## Learning & Memory

- **DARPA SHINE — NEW TO THE KNOWLEDGE BASE (backfill; announced Aug 2026, missed by prior scans).** Special Notice DARPA-SN-26-111 (Info session Aug 25, 2026) → Disruption Opportunity DARPA-PA-25-07-06 (posted Aug 28, 2026; question cutoff Oct 2; **proposals due Oct 9, 2026**; planned start Jan 2027). DSO vehicle (Other Transaction prototype, ≤$2M/award, 18-month synchronized program, month-9 evaluation). Objective: selectively induce *adaptive* neuroplasticity within targeted, dysfunctional neural circuits while minimizing effects in surrounding tissue — a precision alternative to current TBI treatments that "activate large areas of the brain instead of specific circuits in need of repair." Two tracks: Track A (translationally mature, therapeutic index ≥5, quantitative clinical biomarkers e.g. fMRI/PET, aimed at imminent clinical trials) and Track B (foundational/high-risk, animal-ready, >90% spatial-containment threshold). Modality-agnostic across five domains: molecular engineering, neuromodulation, biomaterials, behavioral interventions, synthetic biology. **Scope is explicitly medical recovery from neural injury, not human enhancement** — reinforces the standing bottom line. (Corroborated: Inside BCI Aug 11, 2026; military.com Aug 18, 2026; cleat.ai listing; SAM.gov verified live — DARPA-PA-25-07 Amendment 1 updated Sept 25, 2026. SHINE detail text read via solicitation mirror; verify against SAM.gov before citing formally.)
- Note: Inside BCI flags that some early coverage mischaracterized SHINE as a near-infrared light program — that framing came from a separate DARPA program, not the SHINE notice. Treat NIR-light claims about SHINE as misreporting.

## Cognitive Performance

- **ctVNS in SOF operators — NULL RESULT, peer-reviewed (backfill; *Frontiers in Physiology*, published Feb 26, 2025 — missed by earlier scans).** 35 male SOF / SOF-support operators, single-blind sham-controlled: 2×2-min gammaCore Sapphire ctVNS at 19:00h, then overnight total sleep deprivation with PVT, SynWin multitasking, stop-signal task every 3h, plus VR close-quarter-battle test. Sleep deprivation was successfully induced (all tasks declined, p<0.001), but **no significant ctVNS effects on any cognitive task or the VR operational task vs sham**; stimulation intensity trended toward *worse* SynWin performance. Authors: 2×2-min protocol may be insufficient, and effects may follow an inverted-U dose-response — their intensities were higher than the 2021 Air Force study (McKinley et al., *Communications Biology* 2021) that found benefits. Significance: the VNS evidence base is mixed and protocol-sensitive; this is the closest thing to a negative replication in an elite-operator sample, and directly bears on open question #4 (any fielded/operational use of tcVNS in training — answer leans further toward "not yet, and the data don't yet justify it").
- **Resilience genetics — backfill (USARIEM / MIT Lincoln Lab).** Lieberman/Petrovick work announced Sept 2025: SERE-course volunteers showed SNP rs4251417 (serotonin-transporter variant) correlated with greater *increase* in self-rated resilience under extreme stress; variant present in 15% of volunteers vs 9% general population (n=73 SERE trainees; cortisol ~10× baseline in captivity phase). A companion 800-candidate SF assessment study (47 genes) found several variants associated with cognitive performance/resilience scores, with ancestry-dependent effects. Preliminary, selection-oriented, not intervention — one-line record only. Cross-ref below.

## Cognitive Performance / Sleep

- **RESTORE — BACKFILL, NEW TO THE KNOWLEDGE BASE (DARPA DSO, BAA HR001125S0012; proposals due Jun 18, 2025).** Aim: "precision control of sleep macro- and micro-architectures to optimize cognitive performance" during the combat-typical restriction of ≤3 hours of sleep per day. Approach: multimodal, multitarget, non-invasive neuromodulation methods to repair disrupted sleep architectures caused by sleep restriction. Test protocol in the BAA FAQ: ≤3h sleep per 24-hour period for ≥3 consecutive days (no preclinical animal models). Rationale given in the BAA: hypnotics and stimulants have significant operational drawbacks (impaired cognition, rebound insomnia, misuse potential). Read: optimize the *restorative value of the sleep that happens*, not replace sleep — restorative framing, consistent with the standing line. Industry day March 14, 2025 (Military Aerospace); DARPA-hosted FAQ on darpa.mil (PDF: darpa-program-restore-faq.pdf); University of Utah research-office listing confirms BAA HR001125S0012, due Jun 18, 2025. Note: RESTORE sits in the same portfolio lineage as WRAIR's TES slow-wave work (Hughes/Doty — inducing slow waves to raise the restorative value of short sleep windows) and as AWARE (photopharmacology for on-demand alertness that preserves later sleep): the three programs together read as DARPA/USAMRDC converging on a single doctrine — *sleep is load-bearing; engineer around it, don't substitute it*.
- **New peer-reviewed: prior-night sleep skews concussion test scores.** Sharafkhaneh, A., Werner, J. K., Jr., & BaHammam, A. (2026), *J Clin Sleep Med* 22(1), Article 108 ("Lose sleep, lose score? Interpreting computerized neurocognitive testing after concussion") — commentary on Aderman et al.'s CARE Consortium analysis of service-academy cadets/midshipmen. Shorter prior-night sleep ↔ poorer ImPACT verbal/visual memory, visual motor speed, reaction time across baseline and post-injury assessments; a complementary 25,000+ ImPACT-record analysis found sleep hours barely moved baseline composite scores but meaningfully shifted post-injury memory, impulse control, and symptom severity. Uninjured brains compensate; injured brains amplify the sleep-loss cost. Partial r² < 0.05 (real but modest). Confounds not captured: sleep quality, chronic restriction, circadian phase, caffeine/meds (single self-report item). Million Veteran Program data reinforce: reduced sleep ↔ worse subjective cognitive symptoms in a post-deployment cohort. Proposed protocol: document sleep before every computerized test; reinterpret abnormal scores after <6h sleep; retest after a full night before duty/activity clearance.

## Cross-references (per link discipline)

- RESTORE: precision control of sleep macro/micro-architecture → sleep consolidation (Diekelmann & Born 2010): **direct** — the program manipulates the same slow-wave/consolidation machinery the Yates research cites as the substrate of overnight memory. First DARPA KB program aimed at *engineering the consolidation process itself* rather than countering fatigue pharmacologically.
- RESTORE ↔ AWARE: **convergent** — same fatigue-management doctrine from two directions: AWARE makes wakefulness reversibly titratable so sleep afterward is possible; RESTORE raises the restorative value of the sleep that does happen. Both are engineered as adjuncts to sleep, not substitutes — the Oct 1, 2026 caffeine meta-analysis's core warning, institutionalized as design philosophy.
- RESTORE ↔ iCanStudy "Recover" dimension: **convergent** — "Recover" treats sleep as the load-bearing input to encoding/retrieval; RESTORE is that principle at program scale (≤3h restriction test protocol is the explicit constraint).
- Sharafkhaneh/Werner/BaHammam concussion commentary: sleep as a measurable, modifiable confound in cognitive assessment → iCanStudy self-management dimension (metacognitive monitoring — *record the precondition, don't trust the score blind*): **convergent**. Practical for the user's own study measurement: any timed cognitive benchmark (practice tests, drills) taken after short sleep is a noisy instrument.
- Sleep–TBI bidirectional loop (glymphatic disruption, aquaporin-4 mislocalization) → UC Berkeley tau/slow-wave finding (Sharon et al., *Nat Neurosci* 2026, filed Oct 6): **convergent** — two independent lines tying disrupted sleep physiology to impaired clearance and degraded consolidation, one in preclinical-aging, one in injury.

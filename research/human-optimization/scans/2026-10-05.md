## Learning & Memory

- **Warfighter Brain Fitness Study — NEW TO THE KNOWLEDGE BASE (backfill; published *Military Medicine* 2025, missed by prior scans).** Led by Applied Research Associates (Leanne Young, lead researcher) with the UT Dallas Center for BrainHealth and Posit Science: 406 healthy National Guard servicemembers in a multi-arm study with alternating sequences of 90 days of **BrainHQ** (bottom-up, adaptive perceptual/cognitive exercises) and **SMART** (Strategic Memory Advanced Reasoning Training — top-down, strategy-based executive-function training: focus, reasoning, decision-making, mental flexibility) plus waitlist control arms. Efficacy measured on the BrainHealth Index (BHI), a 22-multidomain-assessment composite. Participants benefited from either training; improvements across all measures including cognitive performance and psychological wellbeing. Authors' framing: "left of boom" (build cognitive resilience ahead of threat) and "right of boom" (faster return to readiness); cognitive training "ready for similar deployment" alongside stress management, mindfulness, and sleep hygiene. Caveat noted in the index: provider-linked commentary (Posit Science CEO, Center for BrainHealth chief) — the paper itself is peer-reviewed; DOI 10.1093/milmed/usaf292. This is a *training intervention* on healthy warfighters, not stimulation or pharmacology — the only item in the KB so far that tests a behavioral cognitive program on the military population directly.

## Cognitive Performance

- **Neurable / Air Force EEG cognitive-fitness tracker — NEW TO THE KNOWLEDGE BASE (backfill; announced Jan 2026 via Army Times).** $1.2M project: Neurable (consumer neurotech startup) partnered with the Air Force / AFRL 711th Human Performance Wing (William Aue, cognitive neuroscience section chief) to study whether fabric-EEG headphones can track service members' cognitive fitness — the "Garmin for the brain" framing (Space Force uses Garmin watches for physical fitness). Builds on Neurable's $500 Master & Dynamic consumer headphones with fabric EEG sensors mapping brainwaves to focus ground-truth markers. The Air Force study will measure cognitive performance with and without pressure — explicitly **memory skill with and without sleep deprivation** — against brainwaves to identify signatures of optimal/suboptimal performance; the intervention is **neurofeedback**: alerting operators to brain-signal changes so they learn to adjust behavior and brainwaves to hit peak performance. Two-year development timeline (i.e., ~through early 2028). Ethical framing in the same article: Giordano (NDU/Georgetown) on informed consent and neural-data privacy; Neurorights Foundation cautions on neurofeedback altering brainwaves vs. physical training, and on cognitive-fitness standards risking discrimination if a warfighter's brain lacks the plasticity for the desired state. Non-invasive, training-based, voluntary-readiness framing — consistent with the standing bottom line, but the neurofeedback mechanism is the closest the KB gets to a deployable "performance" BCI outside stimulation research.

## Athleticism & Physical Performance

- **IBEX exoskeleton — backfill (announced May 2026, USAMRDC; missed by prior scans).** The Intrepid Battlefield EXoskeleton (IBEX Mk2) is a shoulder-to-foot brace developed with Brooke Army Medical Center for battlefield lower-limb injuries (tibia fractures, torn knee ligaments, high-grade ankle sprains, foot fractures — the most common but survivable combat injuries). Lets walking wounded stand, walk, and keep firing when evacuation is unavailable/delayed, reducing the 2–4 troops needed for litter carry. **Rehabilitative/mobility, not enhancement** — logged here, classified adjacent. (C4ISRNet, May 29, 2026.)

## Nutrition / Sleep (minor)

- **Spanish Army beverage co-consumption study** (*Nutrients*, published Sept 7, 2026; n=1,155 active-duty Spanish Army personnel): higher-frequency caffeinated/energy-drink/SSB co-consumption profile cross-sectionally associated with ~13.8 min/night less sleep, lower sleep efficiency, and greater habitual physical fatigue. Self-report, cross-sectional — causality unresolved. One-line record; supports the KB's standing "stimulants are adjuncts, and chronic high-dose use is counterproductive" line (cf. the Navy stimulants study and Oct 1 caffeine meta-analysis).

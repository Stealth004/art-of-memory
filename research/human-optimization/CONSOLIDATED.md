# Human Optimization Research — Consolidated Knowledge Base

Living document. Seeded 2026-10-02 from the first deep-research scan; updated by each daily scan. Dated scans live in `scans/`; this file holds the durable, cross-referenced synthesis.

Companion: the Art of Memory research at `~/workspace/research_notes/art-of-memory-yates-20261002-1652/report.md`.

## Standing bottom line

The verifiable public record shows a large, unclassified U.S. military human-optimization portfolio — **restorative and optimizing, not "super soldier."** No verified record exists of any operational soldier-enhancement program. Social-media "super soldier" claims are speculation unless tied to a primary record. Hold this line in every update.

## Domain 2 — Cognitive Performance

| Program | Agency | Status | Key result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sleep program (2B-Alert, sleep banking, SAFTE/UMP) | WRAIR (Army) | Active, continuous since 1950s | Personalized caffeine dosing held alertness ~80% of target window in 62-h SD study; banked sleep boosts effectiveness |
| Stimulant policy ("go pills") | USAF | Active | Dextroamphetamine + modafinil authorized, voluntary, ≤400 mg/24h; Dutch AF RCTs 2023–24 confirm vigilance gains; *Frontiers in Nutrition* meta-analysis (Oct 1, 2026, 7 RCTs): caffeine SMD 0.73 vigilance, 0.54 complex cognition, 0.45 military task performance — adjunct to, not substitute for, sleep |
| AWARE (Alert WARfighter Enablement) | DARPA BTO | Announced May 2024 (DARPA-PS-24-17), active; WRAIR hosted six-month performer review June 2025 | Photoswitchable dextroamphetamine ("PhotoDex") + wearable NIR (750–900 nm) emitters: spatially selective prefrontal activation (exec function/working memory), avoiding amygdala/striatum; temporal NIR pulsing titrates dose and enables on-demand de-transition so restorative sleep is possible. 3 phases (15/9/12 mo); two technical areas (molecules; transducers); ELSI plan required. PM Pedro Irazoqui (same PM as Firefox). Alertness restoration under SD, not baseline enhancement (backfill added to KB 2026-10-07; corroborated Military Aerospace / New Scientist / Aviationist / DVIDS — solicitation specifics still to verify against DARPA primary) |
| RESTORE (Reengineering Enabling Sleep Transitions in Operationally Restrictive Environments) | DARPA DSO | BAA HR001125S0012 (2025); proposals due Jun 18, 2025; industry day Mar 14, 2025 | Multimodal, multitarget, non-invasive neuromodulation for **precision control of sleep macro/micro-architectures** to optimize cognitive performance at ≤3h sleep/day (combat-typical restriction; BAA test protocol: ≤3h/24h for ≥3 consecutive days). Goal: repair disrupted sleep architectures, raising the *restorative value of the sleep that happens* — not replacing sleep. BAA rationale: hypnotics/stimulants have impaired-cognition, rebound-insomnia, and misuse drawbacks. Complements AWARE and WRAIR's TES slow-wave work (Hughes/Doty) in a shared doctrine: sleep is load-bearing, engineer around it, don't substitute it (added to KB 2026-10-08; corroborated darpa.mil BAA FAQ PDF, Military Aerospace, U. Utah research office) |
| Neurable / Air Force EEG cognitive-fitness tracker | AFRL 711 HPW + Neurable ($1.2M project, Jan 2026) | Development, ~2-yr timeline | Non-invasive neurofeedback: fabric-EEG headsets measure cognitive performance (memory skill) with/without pressure and sleep deprivation; alerts train operators to self-regulate into peak states. Ethical framing in same coverage: informed consent, neural-data privacy (Giordano/Neurorights). Non-invasive, training-based |
| Warfighter Brain Fitness Study | ARA / UT Dallas Center for BrainHealth / Posit Science | Published *Military Medicine* 2025 (DOI 10.1093/milmed/usaf292) | 406 National Guard, 90 days alternating BrainHQ + SMART strategy training vs waitlist: BrainHealth Index gains in cognitive performance + psychological wellbeing; authors say cognitive training "ready for similar deployment." Only KB item testing a behavioral cognitive program on healthy warfighters directly |
| VNS for fatigue (ctVNS/taVNS) | AFRL 711 HPW + others | Research, mixed evidence | 2021: gammaCore ctVNS improved focus/multitasking in 34-h sleep deprivation, USAF personnel (McKinley et al., *Communications Biology*). **2025 counter-data: 2×2-min ctVNS showed NO effect vs sham on PVT/SynWin/SST or VR CQB in 35 SOF operators (*Frontiers in Physiology*, Feb 26, 2025); higher intensities trended worse (possible inverted-U).** taVNS: modest vigilance-decrement reduction in field training (*Cognitive Research*, 2026). Protocol-sensitive; no fielded system |
| Resilience genetics (rs4251417) | USARIEM / MIT Lincoln Lab (Lieberman, Petrovick) | Preliminary research | SERE volunteers: serotonin-transporter variant rs4251417 correlated with greater *increase* in self-rated resilience under extreme stress (15% of volunteers vs 9% general population; n=73, 2025). 800-candidate SF assessment study (47 genes) found several ancestry-dependent associations with cognitive performance/resilience. Selection-oriented, not intervention |
| tDCS | AFRL 711 HPW | Research stage | Vigilance-decrement mitigation (DTIC 2015); no fielded system |
| CSF2 resilience | Army | Active since 2008 | Five-dimension resilience training |

### Context events (not programs)

- **2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (announced Oct 5, 2026) → optogenetics.** Deisseroth (Stanford), Hegemann (Humboldt), Nagel (Würzburg); "discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and optogenetics." Experimental substrate under the Domain 1 program entries (RAM, TNT target the same plasticity/memory machinery by other means). Says nothing about mnemonic efficacy — speculation flag: do not let it feed "super soldier" narratives.

## Speculation watchlist

- "Go pill" folklore — policy is regulated and voluntary.
- Corporate PR amplifying real papers — cite the paper, not the PR (e.g., NRx Defense's SPARC-TMS contract announcement; electroCore on the tcVNS paper).

Cites a "Military Technology Quarterly 2025" source line that does not check out.
- YouTube "DARPA Found a Way to Replace Sleep" (The Pushpak, ~Sept 2026): frames RESTORE as acoustic stimulation "compressing eight hours of deep glymphatic brain cleaning into two hours" and presents the historical Continuous Assisted Performance (CAP) primate pharmacology program with "proved 100% restoration" certainty. The BAA record supports RESTORE only as *precision control of sleep architecture under restriction* — no sleep replacement, no 8→2h compression claim. CAP marked historical; no KB entry without primary sourcing.
- strategicstudyindia.com (Sept 2026) "DARPA Backs 'Smart' Red Blood Cells" — names no DARPA program number; PR-grade, not added to KB without a primary source. Watch for a named program.

## Standing bottom line

The verifiable public record shows a large, unclassified U.S. military human-optimization portfolio — **restorative and optimizing, not "super soldier."** No verified record exists of any operational soldier-enhancement program. Social-media "super soldier" claims are speculation unless tied to a primary record. Hold this line in every update.

## Domain 1 — Learning & Memory

| Program | Agency | Status | Key result |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNT (Targeted Neuroplasticity Training) | DARPA | 2016–~2021 | tcVNS improved Arabic vocabulary recall vs sham (P=.025), n=36, DLI 2024 (*Scientific Reports*) |
| RAM (Restoring Active Memory) | DARPA | 2013– | Closed-loop stimulation on predicted encoding failure (2017); hippocampal prosthesis up to ~37% STM gain (~2018); ~19% TBI (2023); ~28% epilepsy cohort (2024) |
| AugCog | DARPA | 2001–~2006 | Cognitive-state-adaptive interfaces (Lockheed Martin PACE, 2006) |
| Firefox | DARPA (BTO) | 2026–, solicitation DARPA-PS-26-137; abstracts due **Oct 19, 2026** | Non-invasive optical BCI: detect neural membrane micro-movement with light beams; 36-mo program, target 100 channels, 50-µm spatial / 100-µs temporal resolution (announced Aug 2026). Technical hurdles named in the solicitation: dynamic rastering/scanning of the coherent optical signal across regions, and non-digital data-processing architectures to handle scaling data rates — recording physics demonstrated, scaling problems are the actual challenge |
| D-PECHE | DARPA | Announced ~May 2026 | DNA-Protein Epigenetic Chemistry Engineering: reversible new-to-nature base modifications for cognitive enhancement/biothreat/regenerative medicine (seen only in secondary outlets quoting the special notice; verify against DARPA primary) |
| SPARC-TMS | DARPA → NRx Defense Systems ($11.18M contract, Sept 30, 2026) | Trial enrolling | Single-day full TMS course + NRX-101 (d-cycloserine + lurasidone, NMDA-augmenting) for MDD; trial at McLean Hospital/ASU/HOPE Therapeutics, NCT07227103; military-hospital expansion planned (announced via company PR — verify against trial record) |
| SHINE (Selective Harnessing of Intrinsic Neuroplasticity Engineering) | DARPA DSO, Disruption Opportunity DARPA-PA-25-07-06 | Proposals due Oct 9, 2026; 18-mo from Jan 2027 | Non-invasive *precision* neuroplasticity: induce adaptive plasticity in targeted dysfunctional circuits while sparing surrounding tissue (Track A: translational, TI ≥5, fMRI/PET biomarkers; Track B: foundational, >90% spatial containment). Modality-agnostic (molecular, neuromodulation, biomaterials, behavioral, synthetic biology). **TBI-recovery indication — restorative, not enhancement** (corroborated: Inside BCI, military.com, SAM.gov; added to KB 2026-10-04) |

## Domain 2 — Cognitive Performance

| Program | Agency | Status | Key result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sleep program (2B-Alert, sleep banking, SAFTE/UMP) | WRAIR (Army) | Active, continuous since 1950s | Personalized caffeine dosing held alertness ~80% of target window in 62-h SD study; banked sleep boosts effectiveness |
| Stimulant policy ("go pills") | USAF | Active | Dextroamphetamine + modafinil authorized, voluntary, ≤400 mg/24h; Dutch AF RCTs 2023–24 confirm vigilance gains; *Frontiers in Nutrition* meta-analysis (Oct 1, 2026, 7 RCTs): caffeine SMD 0.73 vigilance, 0.54 complex cognition, 0.45 military task performance — adjunct to, not substitute for, sleep |
| Neurable / Air Force EEG cognitive-fitness tracker | AFRL 711 HPW + Neurable ($1.2M project, Jan 2026) | Development, ~2-yr timeline | Non-invasive neurofeedback: fabric-EEG headsets measure cognitive performance (memory skill) with/without pressure and sleep deprivation; alerts train operators to self-regulate into peak states. Ethical framing in same coverage: informed consent, neural-data privacy (Giordano/Neurorights). Non-invasive, training-based |
| Warfighter Brain Fitness Study | ARA / UT Dallas Center for BrainHealth / Posit Science | Published *Military Medicine* 2025 (DOI 10.1093/milmed/usaf292) | 406 National Guard, 90 days alternating BrainHQ + SMART strategy training vs waitlist: BrainHealth Index gains in cognitive performance + psychological wellbeing; authors say cognitive training "ready for similar deployment." Only KB item testing a behavioral cognitive program on healthy warfighters directly |
| VNS for fatigue (ctVNS/taVNS) | AFRL 711 HPW + others | Research, mixed evidence | 2021: gammaCore ctVNS improved focus/multitasking in 34-h sleep deprivation, USAF personnel (McKinley et al., *Communications Biology*). **2025 counter-data: 2×2-min ctVNS showed NO effect vs sham on PVT/SynWin/SST or VR CQB in 35 SOF operators (*Frontiers in Physiology*, Feb 26, 2025); higher intensities trended worse (possible inverted-U).** taVNS: modest vigilance-decrement reduction in field training (*Cognitive Research*, 2026). Protocol-sensitive; no fielded system |
| Resilience genetics (rs4251417) | USARIEM / MIT Lincoln Lab (Lieberman, Petrovick) | Preliminary research | SERE volunteers: serotonin-transporter variant rs4251417 correlated with greater *increase* in self-rated resilience under extreme stress (15% of volunteers vs 9% general population; n=73, 2025). 800-candidate SF assessment study (47 genes) found several ancestry-dependent associations with cognitive performance/resilience. Selection-oriented, not intervention |
| tDCS | AFRL 711 HPW | Research stage | Vigilance-decrement mitigation (DTIC 2015); no fielded system |
| CSF2 resilience | Army | Active since 2008 | Five-dimension resilience training |

### Context events (not programs)

- **2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (announced Oct 5, 2026) → optogenetics.** Deisseroth (Stanford), Hegemann (Humboldt), Nagel (Würzburg); "discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and optogenetics." Experimental substrate under the Domain 1 program entries (RAM, TNT target the same plasticity/memory machinery by other means). Animal-model research tool; clinical human application essentially nil (one retinitis-pigmentosa patient, 2021). Says nothing about mnemonic efficacy — speculation flag: do not let it feed "super soldier" narratives.

## Speculation watchlist

- "Super soldier" social-media/YouTube claims (skill downloading, secret genetic/pharma enhancement) — no primary record.
- YouTube "DARPA's New Invention Could Replace Studying Forever" (The Pushpak, ~Sept 25, 2026): claims TNT subjects "learned Mandarin 300% faster" — unsupported; verified record is the 2024 Arabic trial (P=.025); the ≥30% figure was a program target, not an outcome.
- MKUltra — real, declassified; interrogation/coercion, not enhancement.
- "Go pill" folklore — policy is regulated and voluntary.
- Corporate PR amplifying real papers — cite the paper, not the PR (e.g., NRx Defense's SPARC-TMS contract announcement; electroCore on the tcVNS paper).
- YogaEsoteric's Firefox piece ("Science fiction becomes Pentagon reality") — sensational but cites the real N3→Firefox lineage; correctly notes DARPA never confirmed the film-name origin. Treat outlet framing as secondary, cite the solicitation.
- Note: Oct-2026 press landscape routinely labels DARPA a branch of the "Department of War" — corporate-PR naming, not a substantive program change.

# Human Optimization Research

Daily research scans on **super-soldier programs and government/military human-optimization research** — covering learning, memory, and athleticism — land here.

## How it works

- Every morning, public sources are scanned for new science and research on human optimization (government and military programs, plus adjacent peer-reviewed work).
- Each scan is saved as a dated file (e.g. `scan-2026-10-03.md`).
- Findings are **cross-referenced with the Art of Memory research** in `../report.md` — the ancient memory techniques (places and images, striking images, ordered retrieval, association, spaced reflection) are mapped against modern evidence on learning and memory, so old and new knowledge reinforce each other.
- Key findings are **analyzed and consolidated** over time into `CONSOLIDATED.md`, a running synthesis that keeps the signal and drops the noise.

## Source discipline

The same standard as the main report applies here: every factual claim carries a source; primary texts and scholarship are distinguished; speculation is flagged rather than presented as fact. No fabricated quotations.
